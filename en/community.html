Community Guidelines

Last updated: 10 August 2026 (includes gentle prompts when someone you reported is at the same event; reports do not auto-unmatch) · AGAIN dating service

AGAIN aims to be a warm, respectful space for mature adults. Please help us keep it that way.

1. Sincerity and respect

  • Use recent photos of yourself; profile information should honestly reflect your situation
  • Speak with respect and gentleness; honor the other person’s boundaries and pace
  • Understand that others may bring parenting, work, and prior life experience; avoid judgment

2. Strictly prohibited

  • Sexual harassment, nudity, or inappropriate sexual suggestion (including unsolicited sexual messages)
  • Hate speech, discrimination, personal attacks
  • Fraud, fake investments, loans, proxy shopping, or other money-solicitation schemes
  • Impersonation, using someone else’s photos, or multiple fake accounts
  • Sales pitches, multilevel marketing, or driving people to other platforms without permission
  • Asking for extra offline transfers or deposits at events, or commercial promotion (including undisclosed extra on-site charges)

3. Safer interaction

  • Early on, avoid sharing your home address, financial accounts, or ID documents too soon
  • Tell a trusted friend before meeting offline, and choose a public place
  • If you feel uncomfortable, use Block and Report
  • If physical safety is at risk, protect yourself first and contact the police if needed

4. Reports and handling

You may report or block from the bottom of a Discover lookbook, a matched profile, the chat ⋯ menu, a post-event light-connect chat, or details of an event you joined. The other person is not notified of your report.

Activity reports (including host no-show) open in defined windows around the event. You may withdraw a report that is not yet closed and submit again; each type is limited to two submissions per case. We review records by hand and may warn, pause Discover, mute messages, suspend activities for a period or permanently, delete content, or ban the account site-wide. Malicious reports may also lead to account action.

If someone you blocked / set distance from, or someone you reported (and the report is still active), is at an event, the app may gently prompt you. Whether to join or stay is your choice, and verified refunds may apply under the Terms of Use. They will not learn about your block or report.

After a report is reviewed or we apply a feature limit, we do not automatically unmatch you. If you want no further contact, unmatch or block yourself.

5. Offline event etiquette

  • Respect the host’s “suggested participant age”; if you are clearly outside it, consider whether the gathering fits (suggested age is not a hard block; you may see a gentle reminder before signup)
  • Arrive on time; cancel early if you cannot attend (cancelling within 24 hours of start generally does not refund points; signup closes 2 hours before start)
  • Respect the nature of the event and other participants, including guardian duties at parent-child gatherings
  • Hosts must describe the program and on-site costs honestly (no extra spend, or a self-pay range) and must not steer people to offline transfers or deposits
  • Check the self-pay label before signup; for self-pay events, pay on site yourselves—do not privately transfer money to the host
  • After the event you may leave an honest review within the allowed window
  • Icebreaker replies should be sincere and short; no harassment, sales pitches, detailed locations, or contact details
  • Light connect is available for 72 hours after the event; the other person is notified only if both of you choose each other. Respect their pace; do not harass
  • “Fit with this gathering” is a feeling hint only; trust in-person interaction and your own judgment

6. Consequences

If you breach these guidelines, AGAIN may take feature limits or account action without prior notice, including but not limited to:

  • A warning or watch record
  • Pausing your appearance on Discover, and possibly pausing your ability to host
  • Muting messages (often about 72 hours): you cannot send new messages; you can generally still read existing threads, and we do not auto-unmatch
  • Timed activity suspension (often about 72 hours): you cannot host or join events and are hidden from Discover
  • Permanent activity ban: you permanently lose the right to host or join offline events and are hidden from Discover
  • Deleting content, ending matches, suspending or permanently removing the account (full-site ban)

We may also cooperate with authorities as required by law. For serious violations, unused purchased points may be forfeited under the Terms of Use.
