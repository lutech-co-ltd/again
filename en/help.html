Matching & Discover

How do I start meeting people?

Browse lookbooks in Discover, then use “See next,” “Save for later,” or “Get to know each other” at the bottom. When both of you choose “Get to know each other,” you match and can chat in Messages.

What’s the difference between “Save for later” and “See next”?

“Save for later” does not notify them and is not a block; after about 21 days they may reappear in recommendations if they are still active and a strong fit. “See next” removes them from your Discover list (you can use “Back to previous” to undo the person you just passed).

How many “Get to know each other” can I send each day?

Each member has 20 free daily likes (“Get to know each other”). The quota typically resets in the early morning local time. After that you can still read lookbooks and use See next / Save for later. Glimmer invites do not use these 20.

What is the affinity hint on Discover?

At the top of chapter one of a lookbook, a short gentle line is generated from public profile fields (such as parenting stance, family vision, lifestyle). Tap “See what you share” to read more. It is for reference only—not a score or guarantee. We do not use private parenting-structure details such as custody or co-parenting.

What is a glimmer invite?

Near the bottom of a Discover lookbook, tap “Write an opening line” to send a glimmer invite (30 Glow Points; note up to about 20 Chinese characters / 40 English characters). They will see you and your note sooner in Discover. It does not use the 20 daily free likes. You may send up to 5 per day.

I tapped “See next” by mistake. What can I do?

After you move on, a “Back to previous” button appears at the lower right. The first undo each day is free; later ones cost 30 points each. Once you start reading and decide on the current person, the undo entry closes. It only applies to the person you just passed, not to “Save for later.”

What do meeting preferences affect?

Under Profile → Meeting preferences you can adjust who you’d like to meet and your area. Recommendations follow those settings.

What happens when I block someone?

They can no longer match or message you, and they won’t appear in your Discover list. Blocking does not notify them. If you already matched and only want to stop contact, you can Unmatch without blocking the account.

Why did some chats disappear from Messages?

If you match and neither of you sends a message, the empty match may end after about 7 days. If you already chatted but then go quiet (about 21 days), the thread may move to “Quiet for a while”; a new message from either side returns it to the primary list. This is not deleting the chat, and it is not the other person unmatching. See the Terms of Use.

After I report someone, will they still be in Messages?

Yes. A report is reviewed by us and does not automatically unmatch. To stop contact, unmatch or block yourself.

Why can’t I send messages all of a sudden?

If the account is muted for a violation (often about 72 hours), new messages are blocked; you can usually still read existing threads, and we do not auto-unmatch. Other limits such as social cooldown may also pause “Get to know each other” or glimmer invites. Access returns when the period ends or after support handles it. See Community Guidelines and Terms of Use.

Glow Points

What are Glow Points for?

They are used to host or join offline gatherings (activity deposit), send Discover glimmer invites, and undo “See next” with “Back to previous” (first undo each day free, then 30 points). Points are in-app virtual currency and cannot be exchanged for cash.

How do I top up?

After sign-in, go to Profile → My points or Top up, pick a pack, and pay via App Store / Google Play.

Do I get points back if I cancel an activity?

If the host cancels: joiners are fully refunded; the host’s Glow Points are refunded if more than 24 hours before start, and not refunded within 24 hours. If you cancel: basic points may be refunded more than 24 hours before start; within 24 hours generally not (to limit abuse). If you already paid to unlock meetup details early, that part is not refunded when you cancel. If you cancel, rejoin the same event, then cancel again, points are generally not refunded (even more than 24 hours before start), to stop cycling seats. If, within 24 hours, others leaving drops the count below 3, you may get a full refund when you cancel. If you leave because someone you set distance from or reported is at the event, related signup points are fully refunded (including early-unlock, if any), even inside 24 hours. If the go-ahead threshold is not met 24 hours before start, the event is cancelled and everyone is refunded; within 24 hours, fewer than 3 people also auto-cancels for safety. See the Terms of Use.

Can I get a refund on a glimmer invite?

Points are deducted when the invite is sent and are not refunded. Check your note before sending.

Activities

How do I join or host an activity?

Open the Activities tab (default sort: soonest first) or start a new one. Hosts can use the platform floor of 3 people or set a higher go-ahead number. Start time must be at least two days (48 hours) later. After the threshold is met, meetup details are revealed to joiners 24 hours before start, on the event page and in My activities. Write a clear meeting point and how to recognize you; you can still edit until reveal. Hosts get a gentle reminder about half a day before reveal. If the host cancels within 24 hours of start, the host’s Glow Points are not refunded. Read and agree to the Activity Safety Rules before your first event.

I don’t want to see a particular event anymore.

On the event details page choose “Hide this activity.” It only affects your own list, does not notify others, and does not cancel a signup you already completed. You can clear hidden items from the activity list.

What if the host doesn’t show up?

After start, joiners can report a host no-show on the event page. When the platform threshold is met, joiners’ points may be refunded automatically.

How do I report an activity issue?

Submit a report from the top-right of an event you joined. You may withdraw a report that is not yet closed and submit again; each type is limited to two submissions.

Someone I blocked or reported is at the event. What then?

When you sign up or after you have signed up, if someone you set distance from (blocked) or reported (and the report is still active) is there, the app may gently prompt you. You can pick another event, stay, or leave. If you leave for that reason, related signup points are fully refunded (including early-unlock, if any), even inside 24 hours of start. They will not learn about your block or report.

What is early unlock of meetup details?

After you join, and before details are revealed to everyone, you may pay extra points to view them early (fee as shown in the app; currently 29 points). If you cancel yourself, that early-unlock spend is generally not refunded. If the event fails to go ahead, the host cancels, the safety floor is missed, or you leave because of a verified distance / report conflict, the full amount is refunded under those rules (including early-unlock, if any).

What is “fit with this gathering”?

One or two feeling-hint lines based on your profile, the host’s, other joiners’, and the event mood. Reference only: not a match or attendance guarantee, and no counts or percentages.

What is the icebreaker?

The host may pick one icebreaker from our question bank; after you join you may add a short reply (about 40 Chinese / 80 English characters). Your reply is visible only to you; others’ replies appear when meetup details are revealed (same timing as early unlock). Do not put a detailed location or contact details in the reply.

What does “on-site self-pay” mean?

The host marks whether the gathering has “no extra on-site spend” (for example a park walk) or “tickets / meals paid by each person” with an estimated range. You can see this before signup. Platform entry points are only a matching deposit; hosts must not ask for extra transfers or deposits.

How is “relationships & being together” on my profile used?

It completes your profile and activity atmosphere hints. The full questionnaire is not shown publicly and is not a hard filter. Your how-to-be-with-me note and questions you like being asked can help icebreakers and recommendation reasons.

Can I change life stage or whether I have children later?

Yes. Update under Profile → Edit profile → Parenting & family. When you switch whether you have children, some questions adjust (for example “if they have children” when you don’t, or parenting questions when you do).

What’s the difference between profile openness and meeting preferences?

Profile openness is narrative and a compatibility hint; it does not by itself hide someone from your list. If you want Discover to show only people with or without children, set that in Meeting preferences. At registration the default is “no preference”; you can change it later.

What are activity credit marks?

They reflect offline attendance and completion, such as “steady host” or “thoughtful guest.” They are not selfie or identity verification; tap a mark for an explanation.

What is post-event light connect?

Within 72 hours after an activity, you may choose whether you’d like to stay in touch with someone. The other person is notified only if both of you choose each other, which opens a capped follow-up chat; you may later agree to upgrade to a regular match chat. This is not the same as Discover matching, and a one-sided choice is not notified.

Will light connect remind me?

About a day after the event, if you haven’t marked anyone, we may send a gentle push (you can ignore it). After a mutual choice, if you haven’t said hello, we may invite once more about 12 hours later.

Can hosts use light connect too?

Yes. Hosts, like joiners, may mark people they enjoyed talking with within 72 hours after the event; a chat still opens only when both sides choose each other.

Why can’t I host or join activities?

If Discover is paused, you usually won’t appear on others’ Discover lists and may be unable to send “Get to know each other” or glimmer invites; existing messages generally still work, and hosting or joining events is usually still allowed. A timed or permanent activity ban blocks hosting and joining. See the Activity Safety Rules and Terms of Use.

Account

Who can register? Must I be 18 (an adult)?

You must be 18 or older. Age is checked from the date of birth you enter during onboarding; anyone under 18 cannot finish registration and is returned to the welcome screen. Please enter a true date of birth. See Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Forgot password or can’t sign in?

Please sign in with Google or Apple. If you previously linked email and password, you can send a reset from Profile → Account & security; otherwise keep using Google / Apple, or email support from Help so we can verify you.

How long can my display name be?

There is a visual length limit: about 8 Chinese characters or 16 English characters (mixed scripts share the width budget). Age is shown next to the name on Discover.

How do I delete my account?

At the bottom of Profile, tap “Delete account and remove my data.” This permanently deletes your profile, matches, and unused points and cannot be undone.

How do I change notifications?

Go to Profile → Notification settings to toggle match, message, activity, and points pushes.

How do I contact support?

In Help, pick a topic to open a prefilled email, or write to again.support@gmail.com. Each type has a matching subject and prompts, and may include login email, display name, member ID, and device OS so we can identify you. Do not paste unnecessary personal data or someone else’s private information.

Still need help? Email again.support@gmail.com (in the app you can pick a topic and a prefilled template). You can also read the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines.
