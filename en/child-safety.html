Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 12 August 2026 · AGAIN dating service

This page sets out AGAIN’s public standards for Google Play’s Child Safety Standards and for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). AGAIN is a dating and social matching service for adults and is not directed at children.

1. Who the service is for, and age

  • AGAIN is only for users 18 or older (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, if higher).
  • When creating a profile, we verify age from the date of birth the user provides. Anyone under 18 cannot finish registration and will not appear on Discover or other public surfaces.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If we learn an account is held by a minor, we will investigate promptly and delete related data as required by law.

2. Prohibited content and conduct (zero tolerance)

Any sexual abuse, exploitation, sexualized depiction, solicitation, or distribution of related material involving children is forbidden, including but not limited to:

  • Creating, uploading, sending, soliciting, or sharing CSAM or suspected CSAM
  • Sexual grooming, luring, threats, or arranging offline harm toward a minor
  • Depicting, identifying, or exploiting a child in sexual content in profiles, messages, activities, or photos
  • Photographing, uploading, or sharing identifiable photos/video of another participant’s child without that child’s guardian’s consent (see also the child-safety clause in the Terms of Use)

Violations may result in content removal, feature limits, account suspension or a full-site ban, and forfeiture of unused purchased points. Serious cases will be reported to law enforcement.

3. How users can report

  • In-app report: use Report on Discover, profiles, chat, activities, and related screens for suspected child-safety or CSAM violations.
  • Email: again.support@gmail.com (please mark the subject “child safety / CSAM” and include time, screens, or other details we can verify; do not attach illegal image files).
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local police or emergency services first, then notify us in the app or by email.

4. How we handle reports

  • Authorized operators may review reports and related records, and may hide or delete content, limit features, or suspend or terminate accounts.
  • For verified CSAM or serious harm involving children, we will cooperate with authorities as required by law, and may retain necessary handling records where the law allows.
  • Details of report handling and general community violations are in the Community Guidelines and Terms of Use.

5. Legal compliance and external reports

AGAIN works to comply with applicable child-protection and online child-exploitation laws. When we become aware of illegal activity, we will, as required by law and the facts, report to local or national law enforcement / regulators and cooperate with lawful investigations.
