Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026 (includes official announcement channel) · AGAIN dating service

AGAIN (“the Platform”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data. By using the service, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and login identifiers (such as Google / Apple sign-in).
  • Profile: display name, gender, date of birth, photos, bio, family and values, lifestyle preferences, and other information you choose to provide (life stage and whether you have children can be updated in the app). If you have children and fill in parenting stance under “as a parent,” it may appear as narrative on Discover and matched profiles, and may inform “why we recommended this person” or compatibility hints (generated from fields both parties have already made public; not a promise). Age derived from date of birth may be used for activity “suggested age” hints and “recommended for you” list filtering; these are soft cues and not a hard signup requirement. More private parenting-structure details (such as custody or co-parenting arrangements) are not collected or edited in the current app, are not shown to other users, not included in the public profile, and not used in current recommendations. If historical data of that kind remains in your account, it stays only on your account and is handled when you request deletion.
  • Location: used for matching and to show a fuzzy area (for example “Taipei City · Da’an District”). We do not show other users precise GPS coordinates or distance.
  • Interaction records: matches, messages, reports, blocks, and Discover “save for later” browsing intent. To maintain the inbox, we may record match status (active / ended), inbox placement (primary list / quiet for a while), and related timestamps. When an empty match times out or a quiet thread is archived, we generally do not delete existing messages; they remain until you delete your account or as required by law. The inbox may also include an official announcement channel (shown as “AGAIN”); we record when you open it only to show read/unread status. It is not a conversation with another member.
  • Activities: events you host or join, reviews, meetup location details (full address is shown only to signed-up participants when disclosure rules are met), mood tags, on-site cost type and estimated range, icebreaker questions and replies.
  • Post-event light connect: people you mark within 72 hours after an activity (marks stay private until mutual), plus limited chat that opens only after both sides choose each other. Light connect is unavailable if you have blocked each other.
  • Gathering preferences: “gathering & being together” fields in your profile (style, hopes, a short how-to-be-with-me note, questions you like being asked), used for activity atmosphere hints, icebreakers, and profile completeness.
  • Reports and safety: reports you submit about users or activities, handling status, and withdrawals. Authorized operators may review related records and apply feature limits (such as pausing Discover, muting messages, or suspending activities). Based on your own active reports (visible only to you), the app may gently hint if someone you reported is at the same event, with a verified refund path if you leave. The other person is not told about your report.
  • Support mail: content you send to support. If you open a prefilled email from the app, the device mail app may include login email, display name, member ID, and device OS to help us identify you. Please do not send sensitive personal data that is not needed.
  • Purchases: in-app purchase transaction IDs, credited points, and points ledger entries (for support and disputes).
  • Push and notifications: device push tokens (such as FCM) and in-app notification preferences.
  • Device and usage: basic technical logs needed for operations and safety (such as crash reports).

2. How location is handled

AGAIN uses fuzzy display: others see city / district labels, not exact addresses. Matching may use coordinates to estimate closeness, but we do not show “X km away.” Public activity areas are also fuzzy; detailed meetup info is disclosed to participants only when platform rules are met.

“Fit with this gathering” and similar hints are generated from public profile and activity settings of the host and attendees. They do not show counts or percentages and do not guarantee a match or attendance. Your icebreaker reply is visible only to you until meetup details are revealed; after that, only signed-up participants can see others’ replies. Public cost type and estimated range on the activity list do not include your personal payment records.

3. Photos and user content

Photos and text you upload are stored with trusted cloud providers (such as Cloudinary and Firebase). Photos are used for profiles and matching and are not blurred or unlocked by a message count. Do not upload images of others without consent, or illegal content.

4. How we use data

  • Core matching, messaging, and activities (including inbox hygiene, empty-match timeout, and quiet-thread archival)
  • Glow Points top-ups, spending, and refunds
  • Community safety (report review, blocking, enforcement, no-show investigations, feature limits; including gentle “someone you blocked / reported is at this event” hints and verified refunds, without disclosing your block or report to them)
  • Push or in-app notices you have agreed to receive (including meetup reveal, distance-conflict event reminders, light-connect nudges, and official announcements in the inbox)
  • Product improvement and support (including identity checks and points disputes; the device may store a local preference to hide specific activities)
  • Legal obligations

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only: with people you have agreed to connect with (such as profile info after a match), with authorities as required by law, or with trusted cloud processors bound by confidentiality.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep what we need while your account exists. In the app under Profile you can choose “Delete account and remove my data.” We will delete your profile, matches, and points records, and remove the Firebase auth account. Unused points expire on deletion. Some records we must keep by law (such as report handling or payment disputes) may be retained for the legally required period.

7. Your rights

You may edit your profile, preferences, and notifications at any time, block or report others, and request account deletion. Questions: again.support@gmail.com

8. Children and minors

This service is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect minors’ personal data. When you create a profile, we check age from the date of birth you provide; anyone under 18 cannot finish registration and will not appear on Discover. If you believe we have obtained data about someone under 18, contact again.support@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete as required by law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email. Continued use means you accept the revised policy.
